# PART 7 · USAGE NOTES FOR THE TEAM

## Tone guide
- **Daniel** · personal · vulnerable · direct · loving
- **Karthik** · technical · fast · solutions-first
- **Juvy** · warm · welcoming · morning energy
- **Mary** · calm · steady · afternoon energy
- **Angelyn** · accountability · gentle · structured
- **Usama** · sales · referrals · alumni outreach

## Formatting
- Always use [First Name] · personalized
- 🔥 emoji = celebration (use sparingly · don't dilute)
- Keep messages under 200 words ideally
- Single line breaks for readability
- Avoid corporate language ("kindly" · "please find attached" · "as per our discussion")

## Don'ts
- ❌ Never use "Hello team" (sounds corporate)
- ❌ Never threaten urgency falsely ("only 3 spots left")
- ❌ Never bulk-send · always personalize
- ❌ Never reference internal politics or behind-the-scenes
- ❌ Never apologize for premium pricing

## Do's
- ✅ Reference their specific name + business
- ✅ Acknowledge what they shipped
- ✅ Celebrate publicly · debug privately
- ✅ Use their language (pulled from cohort calls)
- ✅ End every message with the sender's name
